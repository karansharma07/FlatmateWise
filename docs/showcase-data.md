# Showcase data

The public showcase uses sourced neighbourhood facts alongside illustrative apartments, photos, prices and flatmate profiles. Neighbourhood links are area-level context. They provide neither unit coordinates nor travel-time estimates.

- Bengaluru station names come from the [OpenCity metro dataset](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bengaluru-metro-stations), which credits [OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The HSR Layout road and lake context comes from the [BBMP ward map via OpenCity](https://staging.opencity.in/dataset/81b78513-33ed-48fb-9af3-f33ae3575892/resource/77571c4f-cd91-4197-a883-c1c065a646c4/download/bc9a9494-6f95-4695-bcda-4bafa9eef604.pdf). Whitefield's metro opening and business park are cross-referenced with [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1910674&lang=2&reg=3) and [CapitaLand](https://www.capitaland.com/en/find-a-property/global-property-listing/businesspark-industrial-logistics/international-techparkbangalore.html).
- Dubai area facts are paraphrased from [Visit Dubai](https://www.visitdubai.com/en/explore-dubai/dubai-neighbourhoods). Station names reference the [RTA station map](https://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/public-transport/metro-stations-map).
- The three Dubai rent benchmark records are selected from [rents_by_community.csv](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datadubai/dubai-real-estate-dld/main/data/rents_by_community.csv). Credit: Dubai Data (datadubai.ae), Continental Club Property, based on Dubai Land Department open data. The repository reports data through 29 October 2026 and licenses aggregates under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The selected annual medians and contract counts were transcribed to JavaScript. These historical community-wide registered-lease aggregates provide market context. A room share, a specific home, an asking price and a current quote each need their own data.

[DLD open real estate data](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/open-data/real-estate-data/) offers registered transactions, rents and other property records. It supplies useful market context while apartment availability, owner-authorised unit photos and prospective flatmate profiles require a separate source. No redistributable 99acres feed was verified for this release. Live inventory should come from a feed or direct listings with explicit display and photo rights, a source listing ID, availability timestamp, and a withdrawal process. Flatmate profiles and apartment photos for shared homes should be uploaded or approved by the host.

The showcase gallery uses illustrative Unsplash interiors. Its photos, apartment specifications, household rules and hosts are demo content. The property page surfaces the city and neighbourhood only, and the request includes the selected room and demo host.

The housing assistant turns supported phrases into visible filters and answers apartment questions using the facts shown in the demo. On compatible browsers, the optional viewing-question button uses the browser's built-in AI to suggest questions. Availability depends on the browser and device. The default search and listing answers run locally without an external AI service.
